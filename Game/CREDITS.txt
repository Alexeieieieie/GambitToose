GridFlow (АвтоТрафик)
=====================

Code, levels, drawing and sounds: made for the hackathon. All graphics are drawn
in code and all sounds are generated by tools/gen_sfx.py; the game uses no
third-party images or audio.

Engine: Godot Engine 3.6 (MIT license), https://godotengine.org/license

Font: Nunito (fonts/Nunito-Regular.ttf, fonts/Nunito-Bold.ttf)
Copyright 2014 The Nunito Project Authors (https://github.com/googlefonts/nunito)
Licensed under the SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1.
The two files are static instances (weight 400 and 700) cut from the official
variable font with fontTools. The full license text is in fonts/OFL.txt.
